🌟 Cultivating Psychological Richness

Discover how to make your life more interesting, perspective-changing, and experientially diverse

💡 The Core Idea

Shigehiro Oishi's research reveals that a "good life" isn't just about happiness or meaning—it can also be about psychological richness: a life filled with interesting experiences that change your perspective and expand your worldview.

🎭 The Three Pathways to a Good Life

  • Happy Life: Positive emotions, pleasure, life satisfaction
  • Meaningful Life: Purpose, contribution, coherence
  • Psychologically Rich Life: Novel experiences, perspective changes, curiosity, complexity

The Building Blocks of Psychological Richness

🔄 Perspective Change

What it means: Experiences that fundamentally alter how you see yourself, others, or the world.

  • Living abroad or immersing yourself in a different culture
  • Reading literature that challenges your assumptions
  • Having deep conversations with people very different from you
  • Experiencing major life transitions (career changes, relationships)

✨ Novelty & Variety

What it means: Seeking out new, diverse experiences rather than staying in your comfort zone.

  • Trying activities you've never done before
  • Exploring unfamiliar neighborhoods or places
  • Learning skills outside your expertise
  • Consuming diverse media (books, films, music from different eras/cultures)

🧩 Complexity & Curiosity

What it means: Engaging with ideas and experiences that are intellectually or emotionally complex.

  • Exploring paradoxes and ambiguities rather than seeking simple answers
  • Asking "why?" and following intellectual rabbit holes
  • Embracing the discomfort of not knowing
  • Studying subjects that challenge your thinking

🌊 Experiential Depth

What it means: Memorable, vivid experiences that stand out in your life story.

  • Adventures that push you outside your normal routines
  • Moments of awe or profound beauty
  • Challenges that test your limits
  • Experiences that generate stories you'll tell for years

Daily Practices for Psychological Richness

🌅 Micro-Practices (Daily, 5-15 minutes)

  • Take a different route: Change your commute or walk to create micro-novelty
  • Ask "What would X think?": When facing a decision, imagine how someone very different from you would approach it
  • Random article deep-dive: Read a Wikipedia article on a topic you know nothing about
  • Perspective journaling: Write about your day from someone else's point of view
  • Talk to a stranger: Brief, genuine conversation with someone you wouldn't normally engage with

🗓️ Weekly Practices (1-3 hours)

  • Culture sampling: Watch a film, read a book, or listen to music from a culture unfamiliar to you
  • Skill experimentation: Try a new skill just for the experience (not to master it)
  • Reverse mentoring: Learn something from someone much younger or older than you
  • Neighborhood exploration: Spend time in a part of your city you've never visited
  • Intellectual salon: Have a deep conversation about ideas with someone who thinks differently

🌍 Monthly Practices (Half-day to full-day)

  • Immersion experience: Spend sustained time in an unfamiliar environment or community
  • Challenge assumption: Identify a belief you hold and actively seek out the strongest counterarguments
  • Creative constraint: Create something (art, writing, music) under unusual constraints
  • Solo adventure: Do something alone that slightly scares you
  • Deep learning sprint: Study a complex topic intensively for one day
